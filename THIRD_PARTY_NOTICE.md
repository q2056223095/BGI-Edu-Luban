## LearnPrompt/luban-skill

BGI Edu Luban v0.1.0 is an independently adapted internal-quality-governance framework inspired by:

- Project: `LearnPrompt/luban-skill`
- Repository: https://github.com/LearnPrompt/luban-skill
- License: MIT

Concepts explicitly carried forward at the methodology level include:

- challenge the value of a Skill before polishing it;
- inspect comparable work rather than optimizing in isolation;
- freeze a baseline before changes;
- accept changes only after validation;
- prefer real historical replay over purely hypothetical evaluation;
- preserve validation assets in the repository;
- separate modification and review perspectives;
- require explicit authorization for merge/release/deployment actions.

BGI Edu Luban substantially changes the target context and evaluation model for Shenzhen BGI Education Center scenarios, adding domain/scientific accuracy, education and minor-safety gates, brand/compliance gates, Golden Cases, internal-vs-external release readiness, and an organization-oriented AI asset model.

This notice is included to preserve attribution and make the derivation path explicit.
